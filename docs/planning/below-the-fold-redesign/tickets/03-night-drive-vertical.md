# Prototype Night Drive revised: the road without the sideways scroll

Type: prototype
Status: wontfix

## Question

Does the outrun mood of Night Drive (`prototype/night-drive-style`) survive if the Projects are laid out down the page instead of along a horizontally scrolling road?

Night Drive had the strongest atmosphere of the five and the worst phone experience, and the two came from the same decision: turning the page sideways. This prototype keeps the road, the billboards and the car and drops the sideways scroll.

## What to build

A throwaway branch off `main` (or off the branch from ticket 01 if it has landed; otherwise stub the join with a hard cut).

- The camera lands on a road on the near bank of the Clyde, the Skyline across the water behind it.
- The road runs away from the viewer or across the bottom of the screen; either way the page scrolls vertically like any other.
- Each Project is a billboard: the screenshot is the lit face, the name and tagline on a plate beneath. The billboard is the main thing on screen.
- Section headings as neon signs on poles; the Other Section hung from a gantry or as compact plates, whichever fits a vertical page.
- Three tiers: Featured gets the biggest board.
- The car stays in shot and still reacts to the scroll (rolling, braking, reversing). Add rain, tail-light streaks, headlight cones. Overdo it.
- Palette: pick one of the three options in the spec and note which.
- Desktop first; then check 390×844 is usable.
- Reduced-motion fallback.

## What to report

- Screenshots at desktop and phone, one per Section.
- Does it still feel like a drive without the sideways scroll, or does the road become decoration?
- Does the screenshot read as the main thing?
- What the phone version loses.

## Answer

Not pursued: Neon Signs revised ([ticket 02](02-neon-signs-revised.md)) became the homepage as the Tenement.
